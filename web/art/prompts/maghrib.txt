Landscape 16:9. The attached photographs come from the same shoot as the one to make: match them, use the same man and the same kind of flat, but make a new scene.
The same old Stockholm flat, a few minutes after sunset. The ceiling light is off. Light comes from the window and, faintly, from a warm lamp out in the hallway. Through the window: rooftops and chimneys against a blue dusk sky, with only a thin band of pale orange left low on the horizon.
The man is praying on a small, worn prayer rug laid on the wooden floor. He kneels in the sitting of the prayer, sitting back on his heels with his hands on his thighs and his head slightly lowered, his back to the camera, facing a plain wall, with the window on his right. He is barefoot, in the same dark clothes as in the references. Everyday things around him: a radiator under the window, a wooden table with a glass of water and a phone lying face down, a chair, a coat on a hook.
The man, the rug and the window are in the right half of the frame. The left part of the frame is the unlit side of the room, in deep shadow.
